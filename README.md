<h1 align="center">📊 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard</h1>


![Telecom Dashboard](./Telecom.gif)

## 📌 Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing **telecom customer churn** data. The goal is to uncover patterns and insights that help identify the key factors influencing customer attrition, enabling data-driven decision-making for customer retention strategies.

---

## 🔍 Objectives
- Visualize customer churn distribution and demographics.
- Identify churn patterns by gender, contract type, payment method, and services used.
- Provide actionable insights for reducing churn rates.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Dashboard creation and data visualization
- **Python** – Data preprocessing
- **Libraries Used**: 
  - `Pandas` – Data manipulation
  - `NumPy` – Numerical computations
  - `Matplotlib` & `Seaborn` – Exploratory visualizations

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- **Total Customers, Churn Rate, and Non-Churn Count**
- **Churn Rate by Gender, Senior Citizen Status, and Contract Type**
- **Service-wise Churn Insights**
- **Payment Method Impact on Churn**
- **Dependents and Partner Analysis**
- **Visual Pie Charts and Bar Graphs for Trend Clarity**

---

## 📁 Dataset
The dataset includes customer details like:
- Demographics (gender, senior citizen)
- Account info (tenure, contract, payment method)
- Services subscribed (internet, phone, streaming)
- Churn status (Yes/No)

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Higher churn observed among customers with month-to-month contracts.
- Fiber optic internet users show higher churn compared to DSL.
- Customers without dependents or partners tend to churn more.
- Electronic check payment method correlates with higher churn rate.
